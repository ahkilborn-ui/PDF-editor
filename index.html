PDF Editor

Drop files here, or click Open. Pick several files to merge them.

Opens PDFs, photos (JPG, PNG, iPhone HEIC…) and Word .docx files. Photos and Word files are turned into PDF pages.

  • Text — click anywhere on a page and type.
  • Highlight — drag over an area, or select text and press H.
  • White-out — cover existing content, then type over it to "edit" it.
  • Rectangle and Draw — boxes and freehand lines in any color.
  • Add files — merge other PDFs, photos or Word files onto the end. Each page has buttons to move or delete it.
  • Make searchable — reads scanned pages (OCR) so Ctrl+F finds their words.

Everything runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.
